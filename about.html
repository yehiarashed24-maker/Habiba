About Me

18-year-old artist and engineering student, trained in Cairo, Rome, and Spain (Gravura Taller de Grabado) under Donzi El Housieny, Donzi Art Academy, Dr. Mourad Darwish, Dr. Ahmed Badawy, Galal Gomaa, Massimiliano Locco, Merima Fetahovic, and Dr. Nasser Elgilani.

Featured in Talents on Set, Vintage, Movement, Art of Transparency, and Global Egyptian Youth; and recognized by the Egyptian Embassy in Spain and the Indian Embassy in Egypt.
